# Contributing to Magic IndexedDB

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Magic IndexedDB!

Community participation has been an important part of the project's development, and we welcome developers who want to help improve its quality, capabilities, documentation, and developer experience.

## Ways to contribute

You can contribute by:

- Reporting bugs and unexpected behavior;
- Suggesting improvements;
- Participating in technical discussions;
- Improving documentation and examples;
- Helping identify compatibility issues;
- Improving performance, reliability, and security;
- Submitting fixes and new functionality.

Contributions of all sizes are appreciated.

## Domain neutrality

Keep implementation, APIs, tests, fixtures, benchmarks, and developer documentation domain-neutral. Use fictional data in examples and avoid application-specific workflows, private infrastructure, and internal endpoints. Preserve authentic compatibility fixtures, applicable notices, and intentional project licensing, funding, and attribution references.

## Development philosophy

Magic IndexedDB aims to provide a reliable, intuitive, strongly typed, and production-oriented experience for developers working with browser-based databases.

We value:

- Developer experience;
- Clear and maintainable architecture;
- Security-conscious design;
- Predictable behavior;
- Performance and efficiency;
- Strong compatibility;
- Comprehensive testing;
- Useful and understandable documentation.

When proposing significant architectural changes, we encourage opening a discussion before beginning extensive implementation work.

## Submitting contributions

Before submitting a pull request:

1. Review the relevant project documentation.
2. Follow the established coding conventions and architectural patterns.
3. Include appropriate tests for new functionality or behavioral changes.
4. Avoid introducing unnecessary dependencies.
5. Document important changes in behavior or public interfaces.
6. Explain the purpose of your changes and any relevant design considerations.

Maintainers may request modifications, additional tests, documentation, or clarification before accepting a contribution.

## Contribution rights

Official version 3 distributions are offered under the [Magic IndexedDB Community License](LICENSE.txt), subject to independently existing rights.

The project is supported and funded by Sayou and may also be made available under separate licensing arrangements.

To support continued maintenance, distribution, and separate licensing of the project, copyrightable code, documentation, and other contributions intended for inclusion in the official project require an applicable written Contributor License Agreement before acceptance. Contact the maintainers for the approved agreement and signing process. Do not assume a pull request, DCO sign-off, or GitHub account alone constitutes acceptance of that agreement. Ordinary bug reports, feature suggestions, and technical discussion do not require a CLA merely to participate. Previously accepted contributions are not retroactively subject to the new requirement.

Contributors retain ownership of their original contributions, subject to the rights granted through the applicable contribution agreement.

Until the project has an approved signing process, maintainers hold new external contributions requiring a CLA for review.

You must have the necessary rights to submit any material you contribute.

Do not submit third-party source code, proprietary material, confidential information, or other content that you are not authorized to contribute.

## Security vulnerabilities

If you discover a potential security vulnerability, please use the project's private security reporting channel if enabled, or contact the maintainers privately through Sayou to arrange disclosure.

Avoid publicly disclosing sensitive vulnerability details before the maintainers have had an opportunity to investigate and respond.

Do not include confidential information, production credentials, or other sensitive data in public issues or pull requests.

## Community conduct

We encourage constructive, respectful, and technically focused discussions.

Disagreement is welcome when it helps improve the project. Personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory behavior, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not.
