# Request written authorization

Restricted does not mean unwelcome. We welcome inquiries about related and competing applications, and authorization may be free or paid, depending on the project. No fee, registration, or permission request is required for otherwise permitted uses.

Contact Sayou using the contact information at [sayou.io](https://sayou.io). Include your organization or name, a contact method, the product or project and its intended users, the proposed role of Magic IndexedDB, anticipated versions, and whether the application is internal, distributed, or hosted. Please do not send confidential records, credentials, or sensitive proprietary information.

A request alone is not authorization. Qualifying evaluation remains permitted under Section 6.3; restricted development beyond reasonable evaluation and restricted operational use require prior written authorization. An authorization should specify the permitted parties, uses, versions, scope, and any conditions. Only a person authorized by the relevant rights holder can issue it.
