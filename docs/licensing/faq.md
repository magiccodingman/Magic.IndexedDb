# Licensing Frequently Asked Questions

## General licensing

### Is Magic IndexedDB free?

Yes.

Magic IndexedDB is free for personal and commercial uses permitted under the Magic IndexedDB Community License. Some operational uses require authorization regardless of profit status.

There are no licensing fees, royalties, registration requirements, or revenue limits for uses permitted under the license.

Certain reserved fields and competitive applications require prior written authorization.

### Can I use Magic IndexedDB in a commercial application?

Yes, provided your intended use does not fall within a restricted field or constitute Competitive Use as defined in the license.

You may sell applications, charge subscription fees, generate revenue, and operate commercial services incorporating Magic IndexedDB.

### What if my company becomes very successful?

There are no revenue thresholds, employee limits, valuation limits, installation limits, or customer-count restrictions for otherwise permitted uses.

Commercial success does not independently create a licensing obligation.

### Do I need to register my application?

No.

Registration and notification are not required for unrestricted uses.

### Can I use Magic IndexedDB in proprietary, closed-source software?

Yes.

The license does not require you to publicly disclose your application's proprietary source code merely because it incorporates Magic IndexedDB.

You must retain the applicable licensing and copyright notices when distributing covered portions of the Software.

### Can I modify Magic IndexedDB?

Yes.

You may modify, extend, adapt, and customize the Software for permitted uses.

You are not generally required to publish your private modifications.

### Can I redistribute Magic IndexedDB?

Yes, subject to the applicable licensing terms and notice requirements.

Redistribution does not remove the restrictions that apply to the covered Software.

### Can I build another library that depends on Magic IndexedDB?

Yes.

General-purpose libraries, frameworks, developer tools, and components may use Magic IndexedDB.

The fact that an independently developed library could potentially be used within a restricted field does not automatically prohibit its general development and distribution.

Downstream users remain responsible for complying with the license in their own applications.

## Restricted fields and competitive applications

### Why are some fields restricted?

Magic IndexedDB is supported and funded by Sayou.

The reserved fields and competitive-use restrictions are intended to protect Sayou's ability to continue investing in the technology without having that investment directly leveraged against its business interests.

More information about the reasoning behind this model is available in our Licensing Philosophy documentation.

### What industries are restricted?

The license identifies reserved fields including healthcare, behavioral health, pharmaceutical management, human services, disability and rehabilitation services, elderly care, case management, publicly funded service programs, grant-funded service programs, and related specialized administrative, financial, and operational functionality.

The precise definitions and scope are established in the full license.

### Does my company need permission simply because it operates in healthcare?

Not necessarily.

The restrictions are based on the relevant use of Magic IndexedDB, rather than solely on the industry classification of your organization.

A company operating in healthcare may use Magic IndexedDB for unrelated applications that do not constitute a Reserved Field of Use or Competitive Use.

However, using Magic IndexedDB to develop or operate a healthcare-specific system may require authorization.

### What if healthcare or case management is only a small part of my product?

The restrictions may still apply.

Restricted functionality is not automatically exempt because it is secondary, optional, integrated into a larger application, or represents only a small percentage of a company's business.

The relevant use of Magic IndexedDB determines whether authorization is required.

### What if my organization is a nonprofit?

Nonprofit status does not automatically exempt an operational use within a reserved field.

However, nonprofit organizations are encouraged to contact Sayou regarding individual authorization.

### What if my organization is a government agency?

Governmental organizations are subject to the applicable licensing terms in the same manner as other Licensees, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Governmental organizations seeking to use Magic IndexedDB within a reserved field are encouraged to contact Sayou.

### What if my project receives government funding?

Receiving government funding does not independently make your project restricted.

The restriction concerns the relevant purpose and functionality of the Software, rather than merely the source of an organization's funding.

For example, a general-purpose engineering application does not become restricted solely because its development was funded by a government grant.

### What if my company builds an internal application and does not sell it?

Internal use is generally permitted outside restricted fields.

However, an internal operational system providing functionality within a reserved field may still require authorization.

The restrictions are not limited to externally sold software products.

### Can I use Magic IndexedDB for educational or research projects involving healthcare?

Genuine educational, personal learning, technical evaluation, and non-operational research activities are expressly accommodated by the license.

These permissions do not automatically extend to operational deployment of a restricted application.

### What does Competitive Use mean?

Competitive Use concerns applications, products, services, or material functionality that meaningfully compete with, substitute for, or provide substantially equivalent market-specific functionality to an applicable Sayou offering.

A product does not need to duplicate the entire Sayou platform to constitute Competitive Use.

However, sharing ordinary general-purpose features such as authentication, reporting, scheduling, encryption, or offline storage does not independently establish competition.

The relevant market, intended users, operational purpose, and substitutability are important considerations.

### What if Sayou expands into my industry in the future?

The license includes protections for previously permitted uses that become competitive solely because Sayou subsequently introduces a new product or enters a new market.

Subject to the terms of the license, you may continue using versions released before that expansion for your previously permitted application.

Future releases may require separate authorization. See the [competitive-scope guidance](competitive-scope.md); a later update to documentation cannot enlarge the scope of an older release.

This protection does not override the independently established Reserved Fields of Use.

### What counts as case management, or indirect use of Magic IndexedDB?

Coordinated individualized case or service management is reserved across industries. Ordinary sales CRM, contact management, IT tickets, project tracking, and membership systems are not restricted merely by those functions. Specialized case-management functionality remains covered.

Using Magic IndexedDB through a wrapper, another library, or a general-purpose component to support a restricted application does not remove the need for authorization. Even an EHR's offline preferences can support its restricted application. An unrelated cafeteria or general-purpose product from the same company can remain permitted. General distribution of independent general-purpose tools is permitted subject to Sections 6 and 8; their downstream restricted uses are not automatically licensed.

### Do I own the schema and migration files generated by magicdb?

The license does not claim your models, records, business logic, or ordinary generated artifacts merely because Magic processes or generates them. Section 8.6 permits ordinary generated boilerplate to be incorporated and redistributed without the restricted-use conditions. Copied runtime, engine, CLI, and substantial implementation code retain their applicable terms. This does not authorize using the tool itself for restricted development.

## Requesting authorization

### My project falls within a reserved field. Am I prohibited from using Magic IndexedDB forever?

No.

The license requires separate written authorization for restricted uses. It does not establish an absolute prohibition against licensing applications within those fields.

We genuinely encourage you to contact us.

### Will I automatically be required to purchase a commercial license?

No.

Separate authorization may be granted without licensing fees.

Depending on the circumstances, authorization may take the form of an individual written permission, commercial license, collaboration, partnership, or other agreement.

### How much does separate authorization cost?

There is no mandatory universal licensing fee.

Every project is different, and authorization may be provided without charge where appropriate.

Contact us to discuss your intended use.

### What if my application complements Sayou rather than competes with it?

We would particularly encourage you to contact us.

Many applications within related industries are complementary rather than competitive, and we are open to exploring opportunities to support those projects.

### What if I am unsure whether my application is restricted?

Please reach out.

You do not need to prepare an extensive legal analysis or formal licensing proposal before contacting us.

A brief description of your project, intended users, and planned use of Magic IndexedDB is a good starting point.

### What information should I provide when requesting permission?

Where possible, tell us:

- Your name or organization;
- The purpose of your application;
- Its intended users or market;
- How Magic IndexedDB will be used;
- Whether the application will be internal, publicly distributed, or commercially offered.

Additional information may be requested where necessary.

You should not send confidential records, credentials, or unrelated proprietary information as part of your inquiry.

### Where do I request permission?

Contact Sayou using the contact information available at:

https://sayou.io

### Can I begin using Magic IndexedDB in a restricted production application while awaiting approval?

Technical evaluation and other expressly permitted activities may be undertaken in accordance with the license.

However, restricted operational use requires authorization before deployment.

Submitting an inquiry does not independently grant permission.

## Previous releases

### What happened to the MIT License?

Previously MIT-licensed versions and other independently licensed materials retain those MIT rights, including previously licensed source or pre-release materials even if no NuGet package was published.

The Magic IndexedDB Community License applies to versions and materials expressly distributed under the new terms.

### Can I continue using an older MIT-licensed version?

Yes.

The new license does not withdraw permissions that were previously granted under MIT.

### Can I continue maintaining a fork of an MIT-licensed release?

Yes, subject to the original MIT terms applicable to that code.

Code subsequently distributed exclusively under the Magic IndexedDB Community License remains subject to its applicable licensing terms unless independently licensed otherwise.

### Does upgrading from version 2 to version 3 change my licensing obligations?

Yes, for newly licensed material. Official v3 previews and stable distributions are offered under MICL; independently held prior rights remain unaffected. Review the terms before upgrading.

## Community and project development

### Is Magic IndexedDB still publicly available?

Yes.

We remain committed to making the project publicly available and sharing its continued development with the developer community.

### Is Magic IndexedDB still open source?

Previously MIT-licensed releases remain open source.

Versions governed by the Magic IndexedDB Community License are described as source-available because the license contains field-of-use and competitive-use restrictions.

We continue to believe in the value of open-source development, collaboration, and transparent technology.

### Can I still contribute?

Absolutely.

Community discussions, bug reports, suggestions, documentation improvements, and source code contributions remain welcome.

Copyrightable contributions intended for inclusion in the official project require an applicable approved CLA before acceptance. Ordinary feedback and discussions do not require one. Contributors retain ownership subject to the rights they grant.

### Why didn't Sayou simply make future versions proprietary?

Because we believe that the developer community should continue benefiting from the technology.

We want Magic IndexedDB to remain accessible, useful, collaborative, and available for developers to build upon.

The licensing transition is intended to allow Sayou to protect its business interests while continuing to invest in and share the project.

### Does Sayou guarantee future releases or support?

The public license does not establish a contractual support or maintenance obligation.

However, Magic IndexedDB is an important part of Sayou's technological direction, and we have a strong interest in its continued reliability, security, performance, and development.
