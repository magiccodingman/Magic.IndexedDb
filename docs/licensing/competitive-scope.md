# Competitive scope and release records

Section 4 of the [MICL](../../LICENSE.txt) identifies reserved fields independently of Sayou's current offerings. Section 5 addresses material competitive use, including specialized modules and commercial substitutes, assessed against actual offerings as of the first official public release of the applicable version under MICL, including released previews.

Generic features, unrelated products, and unoffered future plans do not independently establish competitive use. A new Sayou offering cannot retroactively broaden the competitive scope of a previously released version. See Section 5.5 for the continued-use protection.

The project maintains release-dated offering records alongside official releases. These records are explanatory evidence, not a mutable restriction list. Review the record supplied with the applicable release; later records apply to later releases. If a record is incomplete, the operative license still controls and clarification is available through [Sayou](https://sayou.io).
